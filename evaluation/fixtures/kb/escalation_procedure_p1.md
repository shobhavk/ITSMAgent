# P1 Incident Escalation Procedure

## Notification
For every P1 incident the Major Incident Manager must be notified within 15 minutes of the incident being logged.

## Communication
Open a bridge call for the resolver groups and send a status update to stakeholders every 30 minutes until service is restored.

## Post Incident
A post-incident review must be completed within 5 working days of closure for any P1.
